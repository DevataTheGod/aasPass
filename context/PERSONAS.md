# User Personas (Behavioral)

> Personas here are **behavioral**, not just demographic. Each persona describes an emotional need, trigger moments, fears, likely behaviors, retention hooks, and safety sensitivities.

---

## 1. The Lonely Newcomer 😔

**Demographic:** New city / new college / new job / recent move
**Age range:** 18–26

### Emotional Need
Belonging. Wants to find people and feel connected to their new area.

### Trigger Moments
- Weekend with no plans
- Seeing others hang out while they're alone
- Evening boredom in hostel/PG
- After lecture/class, nothing to do

### Fears
- Awkwardness of reaching out
- Safety of meeting strangers
- Not knowing where to go or what to do

### Likely Behaviors
- Scrolls first, posts cautiously
- Responds to others' plans before creating own
- Uses "interested" before committing
- Uses app for discovery before coordination

### Retention Hooks
- Someone replies to their comment
- Push notification: "3 people near you are playing badminton"
- Seeing familiar names recur
- Safety signals (verified users, public meetup suggestions)

### Safety Sensitivity
**HIGH.** Needs clear privacy cues, safety prompts, and supportive community tone.

## 2. The Spontaneous Organizer 🔥

**Demographic:** Extrovert student/social butterfly
**Age range:** 18–25

### Emotional Need
Social agency. Loves making plans and bringing people together.

### Trigger Moments
- Has free time and energy
- Sees an empty slot in a plan
- Wants to try a new place/activity
- Friends are busy but others might not be

### Fears
- No one responds
- Flakes
- Wasting time organizing to empty room

### Likely Behaviors
- Creates 3x more posts than average user
- Uses "Now" posts heavily
- DMs responders quickly
- Shares plans with friends
- Becomes de facto community organizer

### Retention Hooks
- Fast responses to their posts
- Reputation (view count, response rate)
- Being recognized as "the organizer"
- Seeing their posts get activity

### Safety Sensitivity
**LOW-MEDIUM.** Confident in social settings, but needs guidance for meetup safety.

### Criticality for MVP
**MOST IMPORTANT USER.** Density depends on them.

## 3. The Activity Seeker ⚡

**Demographic:** Sports/hobby/event enthusiast
**Age range:** 18–28

### Emotional Need
Action. Wants to DO something, not just browse.

### Trigger Moments
- Wants to play but has no team/partner
- Heard about an event/activity
- Regular schedule (evening sports, weekend plans)
- Boredom + specific activity in mind

### Fears
- No one interested in their activity
- Not knowing venue/meetup details
- Skill-level mismatch

### Likely Behaviors
- Searches by category (sports, gaming, music)
- Joins quickly when relevant activity appears
- May not post much but responds actively
- Uses app as utility ("need football partners")

### Retention Hooks
- Activity-specific notifications
- Recurring same-time plans (badminton every Tuesday)
- Finding regular partners through the app

### Safety Sensitivity
**MEDIUM.** Concerned about skill compatibility and venue safety.

## 4. The Local Explorer 🌍

**Demographic:** Curious student, new resident
**Age range:** 18–24

### Emotional Need
Discovery. Wants to know what's around and who's interesting.

### Trigger Moments
- Free time with no fixed plan
- Walking around and wondering
- Social media boredom
- Hearing about a local event/place

### Fears
- Missing out on good stuff happening nearby
- Not knowing the "vibe" of places
- Overwhelmed by choice

### Likely Behaviors
- Browses feed and explore tab
- Responds more than creates
- Tries different categories
- May not be daily but returns when curious

### Retention Hooks
- Fresh content daily
- Area pulse notifications
- New communities/events to explore
- Seeing variety of local activity

### Safety Sensitivity
**MEDIUM.** New to area dynamics, needs reliable information.

## 5. The Socially Curious Fresher 🧭

**Demographic:** College fresher / new to social scene
**Age range:** 17–20

### Emotional Need
Connection without awkwardness. Wants structured ways to meet people.

### Trigger Moments
- First weeks on campus
- Seeing seniors active on the app
- During lunch/break with nothing to do
- Late evenings in hostel

### Fears
- Awkward rejection
- Not knowing social norms
- Being seen as "trying too hard"
- Meeting unsafe people

### Likely Behaviors
- Observes a lot before posting
- Uses structured responses (I'm in, Interested)
- Appreciates clear event/post formats
- Values college-specific or hostel-specific content

### Retention Hooks
- Low-friction ways to engage
- College-only filters
- Seeing other freshers active
- Community prompts like "Freshers' meetup at X"

### Safety Sensitivity
**HIGH.** Youngest demographic, needs strongest safety signals.

---

## 6. The Trusted Connector 💬

**Demographic:** Known local figure / senior / club lead
**Age range:** 19–25

### Emotional Need
Community stewardship. Enjoys helping others connect.

### Trigger Moments
- Someone asks for help/recommendation
- Sees a post needing a connection
- Wants to welcome new members to area/college

### Fears
- Platform becomes toxic or unsafe
- Losing the local community feel
- Being overwhelmed by requests

### Likely Behaviors
- Comments helpfully on others' posts
- Introduces people in comments
- Reports issues proactively
- Becomes natural ambassador

### Retention Hooks
- Recognition (badges, reputation)
- Seeing community thrive
- Being valued as a local resource

### Safety Sensitivity
**MEDIUM.** Has local knowledge, can help moderate.

---

## 7. The Community Operator 🏢

**Demographic:** Society lead / club head / hostel admin / event organizer
**Age range:** 19–26

### Emotional Need
Efficient coordination. Wants to organize groups and events with less friction.

### Trigger Moments
- Planning a society event
- Need to communicate with members
- Looking for new members
- Announcing schedule/changes

### Fears
- Low participation
- Members not seeing announcements
- Spam and irrelevant noise

### Likely Behaviors
- Creates community posts and events
- Pins important announcements
- Recruits members through the app

### Retention Hooks
- Community management features
- Member engagement metrics
- Event RSVP tracking

### Safety Sensitivity
**MEDIUM-HIGH.** Responsible for group safety.
