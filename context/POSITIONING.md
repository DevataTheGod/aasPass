# Positioning & Messaging Guide

> This document defines how AasPaas talks about itself. The goal: shift from **functional descriptions** ("hyperlocal networking platform") to **emotional positioning** ("Find your people nearby").

---

## The One-Line Pitch

> **Find your people nearby, right now.**

---

## Emotional Promise

AasPaas helps people feel:
- **Connected** to their local area
- **Less alone** even in a crowded city
- **Excited** about who and what is nearby
- **Confident** to make plans and meet people
- **Included** in local social life
- **Safe** while exploring their community

---

## Problem Statement (Emotional)

**Current pain:**
- "You live in a city full of people, but you spend evenings alone."
- "You want to play badminton, but you don't have a partner."
- "You moved to a new college/city and don't know anyone."
- "There's a café 2 minutes away but no one to go with."
- "Something cool might be happening nearby, but you have no way to know."

---

## Brand Voice & Tone

| Attribute | How It Sounds |
|-----------|---------------|
| Warm | Friendly, conversational, inviting |
| Direct | Clear calls to action, no fluff |
| Local | References to specific areas, colleges, hangouts |
| Encouraging | "Join in!", "Make the plan", "See who's nearby" |
| Protective | Safety prompts are firm but not scary |
| Young | Speaks like a peer, not a corporation |

### Voice Examples

| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say |
|-------------|--------|
| "Hyperlocal social networking platform" | "Find your people nearby" |
| "Users can create posts in their locality" | "See what's happening around you right now" |
| "Content is scoped to geographic area" | "Your area, your people, your plans" |
| "Notification settings can be configured" | "We'll let you know when something's happening" |

## App Store Copy Direction

### Title
AasPaas — Find Your People Nearby

### Subtitle
See who's up for something around you, right now.

### Description (Opening)
> Ever feel like you're surrounded by people but have no one to hang out with? AasPaas shows you who nearby wants to play badminton, grab coffee, study together, or just hang out. No influencers. No algorithms pushing random content. Just real people near you, making plans.

### Description (Value Props)
- **See nearby plans** — Sports, food, study, events, and more
- **Make new friends** — Find people in your college or locality
- **Coordinate instantly** — Post what you want to do and find your people
- **Join communities** — Your college, hostel, or hobby group
- **Stay safe** — Locality-only visibility, no exact location shared

---

## Landing Page Sections (Direction)

1. **Hero:** "Find your people nearby."
2. **Problem:** Scrolling but disconnected. Big city, small social circle.
3. **Solution:** AasPaas shows you the people and plans around you — right now.
4. **How it works:** 3-step visual (Post → See → Meet)
5. **Categories:** Sports, Food, Study, Events, Help, Community
6. **Safety:** Your exact location is never shared publicly.
7. **Join:** For your college. For your locality. For real connection.
8. **CTA:** "Get AasPaas. Find your people."
