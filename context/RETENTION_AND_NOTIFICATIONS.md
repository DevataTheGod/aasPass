# Retention & Notification Strategy

> Retention in AasPaas comes from **ambient local energy**, not from gamification or streaks. Users return because their area feels alive and they might miss something.

---

## Core Retention Thesis

People return when the app becomes their:
- **Boredom opener** — "Nothing to do? Check what's nearby"
- **Local pulse checker** — "What's happening in my area today?"
- **Plan-maker** — "Need people for something → open AasPaas"
- **Social fallback** — "Usual group is busy, but maybe someone nearby is free"
- **Ambient area radar** — Area feels active and alive

---

## Retention Systems

### A. Freshness Systems
- **Expiring Now posts** — Posts auto-hide after their time window, creating urgency
- **Activity pulse widget** — "3 active plans in Bandra right now" shown in app header
- **"Happening in your area now"** — Live section at top of feed
- **Category pulse** — "Sports activity is high this evening"

### B. Social Urgency Systems
- **Limited spots** — "Only 1 spot left for turf booking"
- **Time-bound coordination** — "Starting in 20 min", "Need 1 more in 10 min"
- **"2 more needed"** badges on posts
- **Response countdown** — Visual timer on urgent posts

### C. Recognition Systems
- **Regular contributor indicators** — "Karan has organized 3 plans this week"
- **Top organizer badges** — Weekly local recognition
- **Familiar local names** — Profile shown on repeated interactions
- **Interaction memory** — "You played badminton with Riya yesterday" context

### D. Scheduled Habit Systems (Time-Based)
| Time | Trigger |
|------|---------|
| Morning (8-9am) | "Morning plans? Anyone for running/yoga near you" |
| Lunch (12-2pm) | "Lunch buddies? Cafés near college" |
| Evening (5-7pm) | "Sports/gym/activities tonight" |
| Night (9-11pm) | "Late plans? Chai, music, hangouts" |
| Weekend | "Weekend plans brewing in your area" |

### E. Reactivation Systems
- **"Your area is active tonight"** — User hasn't opened in 3+ days
- **"5 people joined badminton near you"** — Social proof nudge
- **"Need 1 more player in your college"** — Specific, actionable
- **"Riya and 2 others are going to this"** — Familiar names

## Notification Psychology

Notifications are **the highest-leverage retention system**. Bad ones kill the app. Good ones create habit.

### Notification Categories

#### 1. Immediate Action Alerts 🔔
*Highest conversion — notify immediately*
- "2 people near you want to play badminton at 7"
- "Karan: 'Anyone for café hopping?'"
- "Study group forming in library now"
- "Need 1 more for football in 20 min"

#### 2. Direct Social Response 💬
*High retention — user got a response*
- "Riya replied to your plan"
- "3 people are interested in your post"
- "Karan commented on your post"
- "Your recommendation helped someone!"

#### 3. Area Pulse 📊
*Medium retention — ambient awareness*
- "Your college is unusually active tonight"
- "5 new plans around Bandra this evening"
- "Sports category is trending in your area"
- "X community posts have new replies"

#### 4. Scarcity / FOMO ⏳
*Urgency-driven — time-bound*
- "Only 1 spot left for turf booking group"
- "This meetup starts in 20 min"
- "Last chance to join evening plans"
- "Expiring soon: Football plan at 7 PM"

#### 5. Trust-Building Updates ✅
*Safety — builds confidence*
- "Your report was reviewed and action taken"
- "Karan is now a verified organizer"
- "Your locality now has an ambassador"
- "Welcome to [locality] — here's what's nearby today"

### Notification Principles

1. **Be specific** — Not "New activity near you" but "3 people want to play badminton at 7"
2. **Be actionable** — Each notification should have a clear next action
3. **Be local** — Reference familiar names and places
4. **Personalize** — Match notification type to user persona
5. **Frequency cap** — Max 5 push notifications/day for MVP
6. **No generic spam** — Never send "You have new activity" without specifics

### First-Week Notification Cadence

| Day | Notifications |
|-----|---------------|
| 1 (signup) | Welcome + "Here's what's happening in [locality]" + encourage first post |
| 2 | If no post: "Ask your locality a question" / If posted: "Someone replied!" |
| 3 | "X plans brewing near you today" |
| 4 | "People you might know are active in [locality]" |
| 5 | "This weekend in [locality] — check out what's planned" |
| 6 | "You haven't checked in — your area was active!" |
| 7 | "You've been here a week! [Persona-specific insight]" |

## Retention Metrics (MVP)

| Metric | Why | Target (Week 2) |
|--------|-----|-----------------|
| Day-7 return rate | Core retention | >40% |
| D7: % users who posted at least once | Engagement depth | >30% |
| D7: % users who responded at least once | Participation | >50% |
| Notification open rate | Notification quality | >25% |
| Avg session length | Feed relevance | >3 min |
| Sessions per week per user | Habit formation | >4 |

---

## Anti-Retention Patterns

- ❌ **Generic "daily reminder" notifications** — Users tune out
- ❌ **Gamification without social value** — Streaks feel hollow without real interaction
- ❌ **Rewarding passive scrolling** — Should reward posting and responding
- ❌ **Over-notification in first hour** — Feels spammy, increases opt-out rate
- ❌ **Showing same content repeatedly** — Feed must feel fresh
